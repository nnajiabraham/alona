Patient notes, drafted on the device

A clinician records a visit or dictates after it. The app transcribes it and drafts the note in the clinic's own template. Audio, transcript, and note stay on the phone or in the browser. The draft waits for the clinician's review before it can be copied anywhere.

Each clinic, group, or test partner gets its own build from one config file. Pick a tenant and this page takes its brand, as the app does.

    What a clinician uses it for

    Workflows

    A note type is a template: a list of headings, each with one sentence telling the model what belongs under it. The clinician picks one when starting a note. These are the selected tenant's templates.

    Around the note

    Record the visit

    Start a note, set a short reference for the patient, and record the conversation. The hint under the reference asks for something short and warns against full names, dates of birth, and record numbers.

    Dictate afterwards

    With nobody else in the room, the clinician speaks the note and the app tidies it into the template. The default tenant has a template for this.

    Jot while listening

    A pad on the recording screen takes the clinician's own jottings. The draft covers what they wrote first, and treats it as what matters most.

    Review and sign off

    Every draft carries "AI draft · Not reviewed" and the tenant's own warning. The clinician edits it in place, then marks it reviewed, which records when.

    Move it to the record

    Copy and Share appear only on a reviewed note. The text goes to the clipboard or the system share sheet, for pasting into the practice's record system.

    Start over safely

    "Write again" and "Transcribe again" rerun the models, for example after a tier change. If the clinician has edited the draft, the app asks before it replaces those edits.

    What protects the patient and the clinician

    Safeguards

    Nothing leaves the device

    Recording, transcription, and drafting run on the device. The only network use is downloading the models once. There are no analytics and no remote logs.

    Only what was said

    The model is told never to add findings, diagnoses, medications, doses, or plans that were not said, to write "Not discussed." under an empty heading, and to keep the clinician's wording for clinical terms.

    Edits belong to the clinician

    The model's output and the clinician's edits are separate files. The pipeline cannot overwrite an edit.

    Samples say they are samples

    Where a device cannot run the models, the app shows sample text, labels it on the home screen, the note, and the draft itself, and says why.

    The app claims no regulatory compliance. It says what it does. Whether that meets the rules where a clinic operates is for the owner to establish before any real patient.

    Decisions for the owner

    • A regulatory review before any real-patient use.
    • Accuracy measured on real consultations, and a better prompt or a larger default tier if Lite drafts stay weak.
    • Whether the patient reference should be screened for identifiers, not only hinted.
    • Whether the audio is deleted once a note is reviewed.
